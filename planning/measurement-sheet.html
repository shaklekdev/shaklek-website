Trousers

Seven measurements

1LengthWaist down the outside to the hemcm
2WaistWhere the trouser sits, aroundcm
3HipsFullest point, aroundcm
4CrotchWaist down to the crotch (rise)cm
5ThighAround, at the widestcm
6KneeAround, at the kneecm
7BottomThe leg opening, acrosscm

Band or tie position

If the piece has a band, tie or elastic, say where it sits. Always measured from the top, downwards.

Shirt: band atcm from shoulder
Trouser: band atcm from waist
Measure the body, not a garment. Tape snug, not tight. Light clothing, standing normally.
Labels and order follow the tailor's own list, so nothing is translated between this sheet and his cutting.
