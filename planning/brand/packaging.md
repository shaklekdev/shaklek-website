# Packaging — every element, what it costs, and what to order first

Written 2026-08-26 on the founder's brief. **Nothing here is ordered.** Prices
are UAE market ranges for a first small run and need real quotes before any of
it is committed.

⚠️ **Packaging is COGS and COGS is currently the problem.** `pricing-todo.md`
was rebuilt on 2026-08-26 against real fabric quotes and the margin came out
**11 points thinner** than the old model claimed — 57–61%, not 69–72%. The
existing model budgets **AED 2 per package**. Everything below is measured
against that number, because a beautiful unboxing that adds AED 25 a parcel is
a price rise nobody voted for.

---

## The decision that sets everything else: box or no box

The founder's own note asks whether paper wrap is needed "if we do a box —
depends on the box". It does, so decide this first.

| | **Rigid box** | **Fabric bag in a mailer** |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per order | ~AED 12–25 | ~AED 6–11 |
| Says | "this is a gift" | "this is yours, made for you" |
| Reusable by the customer | rarely | **yes, every day** |
| Shipping | bulkier, dearer, dents | light, survives couriers |
| Suits made-to-order | it is retail language | it is atelier language |

**Recommendation: no rigid box.** Not to save money — because it is the wrong
sentence. A box is what a shop uses to make a stocked item feel special. Shaklek
is one tailor making one piece for one person, and a reusable fabric bag says
that better and keeps saying it every time she uses it. It also travels better
and costs less than half.

**If there is no box, paper wrap becomes necessary, not optional** — it is what
separates the garment from the courier bag.

## The list

Ordered by whether the launch can happen without it.

### Tier 1 — cannot ship without these

| Element | Spec | Qty (first run) | Est. AED/unit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fabric bag** | Cotton or linen drawstring, garment-sized (~40x50cm). Logo one side. | 100–200 | 6–11 | **The centrepiece.** Reusable, on-brand, doubles as the "box". Cheaper per unit at 200. |
| **Paper wrap** | Unbleached tissue, ~50x70cm, logo repeat or a single stamp | 500 | 0.2–0.5 | Only needed because there is no box. Two sheets per garment. |
| **Brand label** | Woven, sewn into the garment. **No size on it** (founder). | 500 min | 0.8–2.0 | Woven minimums are high; 500 is usually the floor. See the size note below. |
| **Mailer** | Recycled kraft or reusable poly, AE courier-rated | 200 | 1.5–3 | The bit the customer never keeps. Do not spend here. |
| **Care/ID sticker or slip** | Fabric + care + order reference | 200 | 0.3–0.8 | See "what is missing" — this is a legal point, not a nicety. |

### Tier 2 — the unboxing

| Element | Spec | Qty | Est. AED/unit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Thank-you card + envelope** | A6, uncoated, letterpress or simple foil | 200 | 1.5–4 | Handwritten line per order while volume is low. That is the whole point of it. |
| **Brand tag (hang tag)** | Card, string, logo + Arabic wordmark | 500 | 0.5–1.5 | Hangs off the garment. This is where a QR belongs, not on the label. |
| **Sticker / seal** | Round logo sticker to close the wrap | 500 | 0.1–0.3 | Cheapest possible brand touch. Closes the tissue, seals the bag. |
| **Paper bag** | Kraft, rope handle, logo | 100 | 3–7 | ⚠️ **Only if there is a physical handover** — a pop-up, a fitting, a delivery by hand. It does nothing inside a courier parcel. Do not order for online orders. |

### Tier 3 — later, once there is volume

Ribbon or twill tape · branded tape for the mailer · a second bag size for
trousers vs tops · seasonal card variants · a small fabric swatch card so she
can feel next season's cloth.

## Cost per order, added to COGS

| | Bag route (recommended) | Box route |
|---|---|---|
| Fabric bag | 8.0 | — |
| Rigid box | — | 18.0 |
| Tissue wrap x2 | 0.7 | 0.7 |
| Mailer | 2.0 | 2.0 |
| Thank-you card + envelope | 2.5 | 2.5 |
| Hang tag + string | 1.0 | 1.0 |
| Woven label | 1.4 | 1.4 |
| Sticker | 0.2 | 0.2 |
| Care slip | 0.5 | 0.5 |
| **Total** | **~AED 16** | **~AED 26** |

**Against the AED 2 currently in the pricing model, both are a real hit.** On a
389 shirt at in-store linen, packaging at 16 takes the margin from **58% to
about 54%**; at 26 it goes to roughly **51%**.

That is not an argument against doing it — the unboxing is most of what a
customer can actually judge before they wear the piece, and this brand has no
reviews and no press. It is an argument for **putting the real number into
`pricing-todo.md` on Friday alongside the fabric quotes**, so the ladder is set
once against the truth instead of twice.

**The two lines worth negotiating hardest** are the fabric bag and the woven
label. Between them they are two thirds of the cost, and both drop sharply with
quantity.

## Print rules — "print logo everywhere"

Agreed, with one exception that matters.

- **The wordmark is the brand, and it has two halves.** `Shaklek` in the display
  serif with the gold rule under it, and `شكلك` in Reem Kufi beneath. Both, at
  the same lockup as the site header, on every element that a customer keeps:
  bag, tag, card, label. Not the Latin half alone — the site does not do that
  and neither should the physical goods.
- **One-colour printing wherever possible.** The mark is a wordmark and a rule;
  it does not need more, and one colour is dramatically cheaper on every
  process here.
- **The exception: the mailer.** Heavy branding on the outside of a parcel tells
  everyone in the building what is inside and is worth stealing. Keep the
  outside plain, put the brand where she opens it.

## What the founder's list is missing

Five things, in the order they will bite.

1. **A care label or card.** Linen shrinks, creases and softens. Without written
   care instructions the first wash is the customer's fault and the remake is
   Shaklek's cost — and the returns policy already promises a free alteration or
   remake. This is the cheapest insurance on the list. **It is also very likely
   a legal requirement**: UAE consumer law expects fibre content and care to be
   disclosed. Confirm with the lawyer who has not yet read the legal pages.
2. **Fibre content and country of manufacture.** Usually on the same label as
   care. "100% linen · Made in UAE" — check the exact wording required.
3. **A returns/alterations insert.** The policy exists on the site; nothing
   physical says how to start one. One line on the back of the thank-you card is
   enough and costs nothing extra.
4. **A size record.** The founder is right that the brand label carries **no
   size** — that is a considered, garment-first decision and it should hold. But
   made-to-measure customers reorder, and the tailor needs to know what was cut.
   Put it where it does not touch the garment's look: **the hang tag** (which is
   removed and thrown away) or **the care slip**, printed as the order reference
   rather than a size. Then the order in the database is the record.
5. **Something that brings her back.** The single highest-return item in this
   whole list. A card with a code, or an invitation to send her measurements for
   next time. Cost: nothing, it is already being printed.
