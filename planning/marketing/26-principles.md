# The 26 marketing principles (founder-supplied, 2026-08-30)

Source: an Instagram carousel by @entrepreneurshipquote, 15 slides, screenshotted
by the founder and transcribed here **so nobody re-reads 14 images** — that is
real token cost for content that is entirely text. Read this file instead.

Her instruction: check the website against these, and build marketing content
from them.

## EMOTION DRIVES EVERY PURCHASE
1. **People Buy With Emotion First** — most buying decisions are emotional; logic comes afterward to justify the purchase.
2. **Sell The Transformation, Not The Product** — people invest in the better outcome or lifestyle, not the object.

## COMMUNICATE VALUE CLEARLY
3. **State Your Price With Confidence** — present the offer confidently, then give the buyer time to think instead of overselling.
4. **Solve Pain Before Selling Dreams** — people are more motivated to avoid problems than to chase possibilities.

## BUILD STRONGER MARKETING
5. **Marketing Without Psychology Falls Flat** — understanding how people think gives a campaign a stronger chance.
6. **Strong Guarantees Increase Trust** — a clear guarantee lowers risk and makes buying more comfortable.

## CREATE MESSAGES THAT CONVERT
7. **Great Copy Comes From Research** — the best marketing speaks to the audience's needs because it is built on research.
8. **Find Existing Demand** — position the offer where demand already exists rather than forcing interest.

## FOCUS ON CUSTOMER BENEFITS
9. **Customers Care About Results** — people care how the product improves their life, not just what it is.
10. **Authentic Testimonials Build Trust** — real customer stories and genuine faces make a business more credible.

## INCREASE PERCEIVED VALUE
11. **Simplicity Beats Expensive Tools** — staying focused matters more than the latest software.
12. **Become Valuable Before Becoming Rare** — develop useful skills first; scarcity only matters once you provide value.

## POSITION YOUR OFFER BETTER
13. **Saturation Isn't Always The Problem** — many offers fail because the message is not connecting with the right audience.
14. **Value Matters More Than Price** — when people clearly see the benefits, price becomes less important.

## BUILD TRUST AND LOYALTY
15. **Be Specific In Your Marketing** — clear, detailed messaging creates confidence and improves conversions.
16. **Loyal Customers Are Your Biggest Asset** — long-term relationships generate more revenue than constantly finding new buyers.

## IMPROVE COMMUNICATION
17. **Listen More Than You Speak** — the best sales conversations begin by understanding the customer's needs.
18. **Make Your Customer The Hero** — use language focused on the customer and the results they want.

## CAPTURE ATTENTION QUICKLY
19. **Create Curiosity Early** — a strong opening keeps people reading.
20. **Great Marketing Amplifies Great Products** — even an excellent product needs a compelling message.

## THINK LONG-TERM
21. **A Great Offer Can't Fix A Weak Market** — choose markets with real demand before optimising the offer.
22. **Handle Objections Early** — answer common questions before customers ask, to reduce hesitation.

## BUILD LASTING CREDIBILITY
23. **Protect Long-Term Trust** — short-term profit should never cost reputation.
24. **Your Story Creates Connection** — people remember stories far longer than features or statistics.

## MAKE OFFERS IMPOSSIBLE TO IGNORE
25. **Combine Proof, Urgency, Bonuses, And Guarantees** — the strongest offers reduce risk while increasing perceived value.
26. **Strong Visuals Boost Conversions** — professional visuals grab attention, build trust and make the message persuasive.

---

## ⚠️ FOUR OF THESE COLLIDE WITH DECISIONS ALREADY MADE ON THIS PROJECT

Apply them, but not blindly. Recorded here so no future session "improves" the
site into breaking a founder decision or a legal position.

- **#10 Authentic testimonials — THERE ARE NONE.** Production has **2 paid
  orders**. Writing testimonials, inventing faces, or implying a customer base
  that does not exist is fabrication, and on a site taking live cards it is a
  consumer-protection problem, not a copy choice. The honest version of #10 here
  is founder story and process transparency until real customers exist.
- **#25 Urgency — "Never cap ordering" is a standing founder decision.** No
  stock counts, no waitlist, no "sold out" (`planning/tailor-capacity.md`).
  Manufactured scarcity is off the table. Real, true urgency (a genuine lead
  time) is fine.
- **#6 / #25 Guarantees — checkout currently says "No change-of-mind refunds on
  made-to-order pieces."** A guarantee cannot be advertised that contradicts the
  returns policy. The remake flow (P2) is the honest guarantee this brand can
  actually offer, and it is **not built yet**.
- **#25 Offers/discounts — a DET advertising permit is required to ADVERTISE a
  discount in Dubai**, and there is deliberately **no active promotion code**.
  Any campaign built on an offer is blocked on that permit.

**#23 outranks the rest.** Every one of these principles is a way to sell more;
#23 is the one that says what not to trade away to do it. On a brand with no
history, the reputation is the asset.
