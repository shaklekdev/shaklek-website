# TikTok launch

Written 2026-08-27. Research is from three parallel sweeps that night; every
external claim below carries its confidence, because most TikTok "data" is
agency blog content with no methodology and it should not be treated as fact.

Assets ready to post: `brand-assets/TIKTOK/` (five videos, captions beside them).
Builders: `website/scripts/social/tiktok-videos.mjs`.

## Two decisions before a single post

### 1. Business account, not personal. This one is not close.

| | Business | Personal |
|---|---|---|
| Website link in bio | **From zero followers** | Most 2026 sources still say 1,000 followers |
| Trending sounds | **No.** Commercial Music Library only, ~1M pre-cleared tracks | Full library |
| Analytics, Spark Ads | Yes | Limited |

The link decides it. The entire funnel is TikTok to the bio link to
shaklek.com, because **TikTok Shop is not available in the UAE** and has no
announced date. Saudi is reportedly being looked at for early 2027. An account
that cannot link out is not a sales channel.

⚠️ **The "stay personal so you can use trending sounds" workaround is dead.**
Around 25 July 2025 TikTok tightened enforcement so commercially-cleared music
is required for **any account operating with a commercial purpose**, including
a personal account the moment a video promotes a business. So the choice is not
"business loses sounds", it is "business is licensed, personal is exposed".

**What that means for the content, and it is not a small thing:** the videos
cannot lean on whatever song is trending this week. They have to work on
silence, on the Commercial Music Library, or on original audio. Every video
already rendered works with the sound off, which is deliberate.

### 2. The UAE Advertiser Permit. Read this before any collaboration.

Under Federal Media Law 55/2023 and Cabinet Resolution 42/2025, the **UAE Media
Council requires an Advertiser Permit for promotional content published from
inside the UAE, effective 1 February 2026**, paid or unpaid, any follower
count. It is **free for residents for the first three years (2026-2028)**.
Penalties elsewhere in the regime run from AED 5,000 into six figures.

**Posting your own product on your own account is very likely exempt.** Five
independent secondary sources say self-promotion does not need the permit,
conditioned on holding a valid UAE trade licence for the business.

**But:** none of those sources is the regulation itself. The primary text could
not be read (the legal databases blocked it), and one legal summary called the
scope "ambiguous for commercial brand mentions by companies on corporate
accounts."

> **The permit is free. Get one anyway, or get a written answer from the Media
> Council.** A free hedge against a six-figure ambiguity is not a close call.

⚠️ **The moment you gift a garment to a creator, this stops being ambiguous.**
Gifted and unpaid collaborations are explicitly in scope, the creator must hold
a valid permit, and **the brand is responsible for verifying it** and carries
liability for using an unpermitted creator. Before any collaboration, gifting
included: ask for the permit number and put permit maintenance in writing.

## What this brand owns that others do not

Two things, and everything below is built on them.

**1. The garment redraws itself.** Tap "wide" and the image changes to that
trouser, wide. 128 combinations, every one with its own image. Almost
no competitor's site does this, and nobody scrolling has seen a trouser change
its own leg width. The research could not find a named viral trend for
"screen-record the configurator", which is the point. It is not a trend to
join, it is a format nobody else can run.

**2. "Packing your order" becomes "MAKING your order."** Filming an order being
packed is a well-documented format; one founder reported roughly one customer
in ten *asking* for theirs to be filmed. Shaklek's version is strictly better,
because a stocked brand gets one beat (the box) and this gets ten days of them:
the fabric being cut, the seam, the hem, the press, the wrap. **Every order is
a content series.** That is the whole answer to "ten days is slow".

The line that carries it, adapted from a UK made-to-order label that used it
well: *nothing in these pictures exists yet. If nobody orders it, it is never
made.*

## Five content pillars

Niche consistency is officially endorsed by TikTok's own Creator Academy
("Niche is the new reach"), and mixing niches reportedly confuses account
categorisation. So: five pillars, nothing else.

| | Pillar | Why | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The change**, the configurator redrawing | The defensible format. Rewatch-friendly, works silent | ✅ rendered |
| **2** | **The making**, one order, ten days, the tailor's hands | Trust, and it makes the wait the product | 📱 your phone |
| **3** | **The argument**, size charts, sleeves, one price | The ideas the brand owns. Earns saves and comments | ✅ rendered |
| **4** | **The founder**, why you started this | Fastest trust a brand with no history can buy. Lo-fi outperforms produced | 📱 your phone |
| **5** | **The audience decides**, comments pick the combination | Interactive formats get algorithmic preference in 2026 | 📱 needs comments first |

**Dance trends, lip-syncs, hauls and generic day-in-the-life are out.** Dance
participation is reportedly down 64% year on year, hauls make no sense for a
brand with no stock, and the others are declared declining across every 2026
source found.

## The first 30 days

**Judge nothing before 30 videos.** The consistent finding across sources: a new
account is not penalised, every video goes to a test audience of a few hundred
non-followers matched by interest, and follower count does not affect
distribution. But the account needs volume before its analytics mean anything.
Do not delete flops; they do not poison the account.

**Cadence: one a day.** Sources cluster at 3-5/week minimum, 1/day for
quality-led accounts, 2-3/day early to feed the algorithm. One genuinely good
video a day beats three padded ones, because weak completion rates drag the
account's own signals down.

⚠️ **`05` is organic only and the filename says so.** Meta rejects creative that
implies knowledge of the viewer's body, and second-person size references are
the known trigger. If it is ever boosted, swap to the third-person rewrite
(`h01ad` / `h03ad` in `social-playbook.md`). TikTok's policy is not identical to
Meta's, but the same creative gets reused across both and that is how the wrong
version ends up in an ad account.

## What only you can film

Six shots. None needs equipment beyond a phone, and lo-fi reportedly gets
roughly 3× the click-through of studio production with UAE audiences, where 68%
say they prefer raw behind-the-scenes to polished commercials.

1. **Shears through linen.** 10 seconds, close, sound on. This is the single
   highest-value clip you can capture, and it is also the one thing catalogue
   catalogue imagery can never give you: motion and texture.
2. **Chalk on fabric.** The marking out. Hands only.
3. **The machine.** 15 seconds of a seam being sewn, close enough to see the
   needle.
4. **The hem being turned.** Slow. This is ASMR territory and it earns rewatches.
5. **The finished piece being wrapped** in tissue, the gold seal going on.
6. **You, to camera, 30 seconds, once:** why you started a made-to-order label
   in the UAE. Unpolished beats produced here. Do not script it, and do not
   redo it five times.

**The rule for all six: the crop must contain the thing that changes.** It has
bitten this project three times. Sleeves crop to arms. Hems crop to hems. A
flattering wide shot of a model shows nothing moving.

## Captions, hashtags, SEO

TikTok indexes three separate layers and they should say the same thing:
**spoken words** (speech-to-text), **on-screen text** (OCR), and the **caption**
(first characters weighted most). Say the phrase, show the phrase, write the
phrase.

**Target phrases:** `made to order Dubai` · `made to measure UAE` ·
`custom linen shirt Dubai` · `tailored trousers Dubai`

**Hashtags: 3-5, not 30.** One broad, two or three niche, one branded.

```
#dubaifashion #madetomeasure #madetoorder #linen #shaklek
```

**Language.** Roughly 75% of UAE social users engage with English content and
about 88% of the population is expat, but Arabic drives higher engagement in
luxury specifically, and TikTok segments distribution by language signals in
captions, voice-overs and hashtags. **English caption, Arabic hashtags** opens
the second distribution pool at no cost:

```
#دبي #تفصيل #خياطة
```

**Posting time.** Sources genuinely contradict each other, one says Tue-Thu
2-6pm and avoid weekends, another says Sunday 9am, UAE agencies say evenings
7-10pm. Treat timing as a secondary lever: test **7-10pm** and **12-1pm** for
two weeks, then follow your own analytics rather than anyone's blog.

## What the algorithm actually counts

Consistent across every source, in rough order of weight:

1. **Completion rate and watch time**, the reason every video here is under 11
   seconds. A 9-second video watched twice beats a 40-second video abandoned.
2. **Rewatches**
3. **Shares, and a share by DM reportedly counts about double a feed share.**
   Content worth sending to one specific friend beats content worth
   broadcasting. "Which of these four would you wear" is a DM.
4. **Saves**, which rank above likes. Educational content reportedly gets ~3.2×
   the saves, this is the argument for the size-chart and fit explainers.
5. **Comment depth.** A thread beats a single comment.
6. **Likes, last.** Ignore them as a measure.

⚠️ **The specific multipliers in that list are agency-blog numbers with no
published methodology.** The ordering is consistent everywhere; the magnitudes
are not verifiable. Do not build a plan that depends on any single figure.

## Advertising: not yet

- TikTok Ads minimums: **$50/day campaign, $20/day ad group.**
- The learning phase needs roughly **50 conversions per ad group per week** to
  exit, the same wall the Meta plan hit. Purchases will not reach it.
  **Optimise for email signups**, exactly as `pricing-todo.md` already argues.
- **Spark Ads boost a post that already worked organically**, keeping its
  comments and native look. That is the right first spend: put money behind
  proven creative rather than gambling on untested creative.

**Sequence: organic for four weeks, read the analytics, then put a small Spark
budget behind the two posts that already earned their views.**

## Cross-posting to Instagram

The watermark penalty is real and confirmed at the top of Meta, Reels carrying
another app's watermark are downranked, and 2026 enforcement flags them within
seconds. Estimates of the reach hit range 30-70% (third-party, direction
certain, magnitude not).

**Never** download from TikTok or use its "share to Instagram" button. Upload
the original export from `brand-assets/TIKTOK/` natively to each platform, and
on the Instagram copy use Meta's own audio. This costs nothing here, because
the files are generated locally.

## ⚠️ The warning that matters most

**Two of the best-documented made-to-order labels in this research went viral
and then closed.** One built a business on a 3M-view video, reached Nordstrom,
and shut down in late 2025. Another ran 25-30 orders a month and wound down.
Virality solved their demand problem. It did not solve their unit economics.

Shaklek has **one tailor**, and `planning/tailor-capacity.md` puts that at
roughly **11-22 garments a month**. The site promises ten days and nothing in
the code caps orders, which is correct, a made-to-order brand that turns away
demand has given up its one structural advantage.

**But a video that works is a capacity event, not just a good day.** Before
pushing hard:

- Know what the bench can absorb in a week, and who the second tailor is.
- The ten-day promise is on the site. Breaking it on the first fifty customers
  is worse than growing slowly, because those fifty are the only word of mouth
  the brand will have.
- Margin is **57-61%**, not the 69-72% an older model claimed. Growth bought
  with discounts is more expensive than it looks, and there is currently no
  active discount code by design.

## Deliberately not built

- **A cost-transparency video** ("where your AED 429 goes"). It is a proven
  format for one French made-to-order label and it would suit this brand. It
  also publishes your margin to competitors and to every customer, permanently.
  That is a founder decision, not a content decision, so it is not in the plan.
- **The "shhhhh" campaign.** Approved as a direction in `branding/voice.md`,
  and deliberately waiting for a real garment reveal to hang it on. It is a
  spoken idea; it does almost nothing as a still.
- **Creator collaborations.** Not until the permit question above is settled.

## What could not be verified

Recorded so nobody later treats it as fact:

- **The primary text of the UAE advertising regulation.** The exemption for
  self-promotion rests on five consistent secondary sources, not the law.
- **Every algorithm multiplier** quoted anywhere above.
- **Whether TikTok Shop is coming to the UAE.** No official signal exists.
- **Per-hashtag volumes for UAE tags**, needs TikTok Creative Center in-app
  with the region filter.
- **Which trending sounds are live.** Sounds churn in days, and a Business
  account cannot use them anyway. Re-check at posting time if it ever matters.
- **UAE creator rates**, which differ by 2-4× between published rate cards
  (AED 2,000-6,000 per micro post) and claimed real-contract medians
  (AED 1,000). Both are agency-published and unaudited. Negotiate; do not
  budget from a rate card.
