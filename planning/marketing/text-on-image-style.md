# Text on photos: the house style (founder, 2026-11-12)

Founder: "this is the new way to do it" — every NEW picture with text on it uses this.
Reference: brand-assets/ads-2026-11/buttoned-abaya-real/all-included-mar17.jpg
Template: brand-assets/ads-2026-11/buttoned-abaya-real/template-texte-sur-photo.html
Inspiration: shopzavi.me posts (founder screenshots IMG_2169-2177, 2414-2416).

- Full-bleed real photo, 4:5, no frame, no card, no panel behind text.
- Text is SMALL, WHITE, UPPERCASE, clean geometric sans (Avenir Next Demi), wide tracking (~0.22em), soft shadow only.
- Headline split across the figure at mid-height, one word each side ("ALL" | "INCLUDED"), placed in the empty space, never over the face or the garment's key detail.
- Supporting lines: short stacked list, smaller (~17-18px @1080). NO rule/bar above the text (founder 2026-11-12: remove all bars).
- Master logo (white) small, bottom-right. Never type "Shaklek" in a font.
- Photo straight (verticals vertical: measure, rotate), phone watermark ("Galaxy S25 Ultra") always cropped out.
- Grade: luminance-only toward the approved FINAL-post (keeps the real linen colour).
