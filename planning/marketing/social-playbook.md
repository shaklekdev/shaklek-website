# Social playbook, hooks, TikTok, and paid

The earlier tiles restated the website. These don't: every line opens on a
tension the viewer already feels, and the sub-line pays it off. Assets in
`brand-assets/social/`.

Three renders of each: `ig-` square 1080×1080 · `tt-` vertical 1080×1920 ·
`ad-` 1080×1350 (the tallest Instagram allows in feed, so the best paid format).

## Instagram ads, two rules before you spend

**1. Personal attributes.** Meta rejects creative that asserts or implies
knowledge of the viewer's body. Second-person size and body references are a
known trigger. `h01` and `h03` are written for organic feed, where that policy
does not apply. For paid, use `h01ad` and `h03ad`, same argument, third
person.

**2. Don't optimise for purchases yet.** At Shaklek's order volume a
purchase-optimised campaign never leaves the learning phase (~50 conversions
per week to exit). Optimise for **email signups** instead and sell by email, this is already the reasoning behind the welcome-offer flow in
`tomorrow-start-here.md`.

**Placement:** `ad-` 1080×1350 for feed, `tt-` 1080×1920 for Stories/Reels.

## TikTok, it is video, not tiles

Tiles alone will not perform. But you already own the one asset that makes
good TikTok: imagery of a garment **changing shape**. Nobody scrolling has
seen a trouser redraw itself.

### 1. "Watch the trousers change", highest priority

Slideshow, 4 images, ~0.6s each, on the beat.
Order: `straight:cropped → straight:full → wide:cropped → wide:full`
(the four cells of `p-wide-leg-trousers-shapes.png`, posted individually).

- On-screen text, frame 1: **"you can change the shape"**
- Frame 4: **"then a tailor makes that one"**
- Caption: `Same trouser. Four ways to cut it. You pick, we make that one. 🇦🇪`

Why it works: it is a visual reveal, not an ad. The change *is* the payoff.

### 2. "The sleeves never fit", best cold hook

Open on `tt-h04`, hold 1.5s, then the four shirt shapes.
Spoken or captioned: *"Every shirt I own fits everywhere except the sleeves.
So we let you set the sleeve length, then cut it to your arm."*

### 3. "Nobody is a medium"

Open on `tt-h01`. Then the size explainer: XS to XXL, or send your own numbers,
same price. End on `tt-h08`.

### 4. Founder piece to camera, do this one yourself

No script needed, phone in hand, 30 seconds: why you started a made-to-order
label in the UAE. Unpolished outperforms produced on TikTok, and a founder
face is the fastest trust you can buy for a brand with no history.

### Format rules

- **Hook in the first 2 seconds** or the view is gone.
- Text lives in the **upper third**. TikTok's caption, username and button
  rail cover roughly the bottom 500px and right 180px, the `tt-` renders are
  already laid out for this.
- Post the same cut to Reels. Do not leave the TikTok watermark on it;
  Instagram suppresses watermarked reuploads.
- 3 to 5 posts a week beats one polished post a month.

## What is still missing

Video. Every asset here is a still. The single highest-value thing you could
shoot on a phone: **10 seconds of fabric moving**, linen in daylight, a hem
falling, a sleeve being measured. Motion is what the feed rewards and it is
the one thing the catalog imagery cannot give you.
