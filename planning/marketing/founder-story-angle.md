# The founder story angle (#24), drawn from the actual /our-story page (2026-08-30)

Principle #24 says people remember stories longer than features. This brand
already has the story written, in the founder's own words, verbatim on
/our-story. The job is not to write her a biography; it is to lift what she
already published into places where more people will meet it.

**Hard boundaries, from decisions recorded in the page source:**

- She removed the naming of her own medical conditions from the opening line.
  That was her call about her own history. Nothing below reintroduces
  diagnosis, illness names, or medical specifics. The published phrasing
  ("more than half my life trying to understand my own body") is the line, and
  it is not to be sharpened.
- She kept the technology framing after a reviewer argued against it. It stays
  hers to use. The word "AI" never appears anywhere; there is none in the
  product and the word reads as generated.
- No em dashes. No urgency. No invented detail of any kind.

---

## The spine of the story, as she wrote it

1. She spent years changing everything that touched her skin, and clothes,
   worn all day, could not be neutral in that.
2. When she found a piece that was right, it was too expensive, not her
   taste, or not her fit. Never all three at once.
3. She is a tech girl who loves fashion, and the question she kept asking:
   show me every version of a piece before it exists, then make only the one
   I chose.
4. Shaklek means "your way" in Arabic. The name is the promise.

That is a complete narrative arc: problem lived, gap seen, thing built, name
that means the idea. Nothing needs inventing.

## Where to use it

### 1. The day-7 founder video (posting-calendar.md), talking points not script

The playbook is right that unpolished outperforms produced, so these are
prompts to glance at, not lines to read:

- Open with the question, because it is the whole brand in one sentence:
  "What if clothes adapted to us, instead of us adapting to clothes?"
- One beat of the lived problem, in her own published register: years of
  caring about everything that touched her skin, and clothes were the thing
  she could not control.
- The moment of the idea: I work in tech, and I kept thinking, show me every
  version before it exists. Then make only that one.
- What Shaklek means in Arabic, and why the name is the promise.
- Close honest, because honest is the only proof a new brand has: we are new,
  every order is read by a person, and I read the feedback myself.

What NOT to say on camera: any medical detail, any customer numbers, any
"join thousands", any discount, the word AI.

### 2. A written founder post (organic, Instagram caption or TikTok text)

### 3. The story on the site, one recommendation

The strongest line she owns, "what if clothes adapted to us instead of us
adapting to clothes?", is the thesis sentence above the collapsed letter at
the bottom of /our-story. It deserves to be met earlier: as a hero option
(see homepage-propositions.md, Option A) or as the one quoted line in the
About block of the Instagram bio. No page change is needed for the letter
itself; behind a click is where she put it, on her own reasoning, and it
works there.
