# Who we are talking to, and what makes each of them buy

Founder's brief, 2026-09-12, in her words:

> *"our brand is about skin breathing, quality materials, tailored fit, and made
> in the uae. this is how we differentiate from the market. they're concerned
> about what they put on their bodies, they don't want to wear plastic, they
> worry about their skin breathing and comfortable. also, their shape is not a
> size short, they want clothes that fit every shape of you. also, it's UAE
> made, we support local business, we care."*

This folder had campaigns, hooks, a calendar and an objection list. It did not
have **a person**. Every hook was aimed at the market. These are aimed at four
women, and each one names what she is actually afraid of.

**The spine is the line she already approved on the business card:**

> **Your skin breathing. Your clothes fitting. Made in the UAE.**

Three pillars, in her order. Every persona below owns one of them. Nothing here
introduces a fourth message.

# The four

## 1. Reem, who lets her skin decide

**Pillar: skin breathing.** Late 20s to 40s. Sensitive skin, eczema, or the
heat rash that everyone in this country gets and nobody posts about.

She has changed her cleanser, her detergent, her pillowcase and her water
filter. She spends real money on what touches her face for ten minutes a day.

**And she has never once audited the thing that touches 90% of her body for
fourteen hours.** That is the gap. She thinks of skincare as health and clothes
as fashion, and nobody has ever joined them up for her.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fears** | The itch that starts at 2pm. The rash under the arm by August. Smelling of something that is not her. |
| **Believes** | That clothes are a style decision |
| **We move her to** | Clothes are the largest thing touching her skin, all day, every day |
| **Proof we own** | 100% linen. A plant. Not a blend, not "linen-look", not 55/45. |

**Hooks**

- *Check the label on what you are wearing right now. If it says polyester, you are wearing plastic in 44 degrees.*
- *You changed your cleanser. You changed your pillowcase. You are still spending fourteen hours a day in plastic.*
- *Sweat does not smell. Polyester does.*
- *Linen is a plant. Polyester is petroleum. That is the entire argument.*
- *Your skin is an organ. Dress it like one.*

⚠️ **Stay on physical comfort and fibre facts. Do not make a medical claim.**
Not "cures eczema", not "hypoallergenic", not "prevents rash". Linen is
breathable, absorbent and a plant fibre. That is enough and it is defensible.
The founder's own story on `/our-story` is this persona, and her published
phrasing names no diagnosis. Neither do we.

## 2. Aisha, who has never been a medium

**Pillar: clothes fitting.** This is the persona the "shape of you" line is for.

A 10 on top and a 14 below. Or tall with a short torso. Or a waist two sizes
off her hips. She owns clothes she has never worn, and she has a tailor already,
because **she alters almost everything she buys.**

She does not need convincing that sizes fail her. She has known since she was
fifteen. What she has never been offered is the alternative.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fears** | The changing room. Buying online and it arriving wrong. Being told to size up. |
| **Believes** | That the compromise is normal and the alterations are her problem |
| **We move her to** | She is already paying for tailoring. She is just paying for it second, on top of a garment that did not fit. |
| **Proof we own** | Her own measurements or a standard size, **at the same price**. Nobody upcharges her for her body. |

### ⚠️ The only real customer evidence in this file

**Founder, 2026-09-12, from feedback she collected from many people:**

> *"for shirts, often for ladies, the shirt would fit but on the chest it
> doesn't. for pants, the hips fit, the waist never does, or the waist does but
> the length doesn't."*

**The pattern is not "nothing fits". It is that nothing fits in TWO PLACES AT
ONCE**, and that is precisely what a size chart causes: one set of proportions
applied to every body. Everything on this page should be built on that sentence,
because it is the only line here that came from actual people rather than from
reasoning.

⚠️ **It replaced a hook that had no source.** *"The shirt fits. The sleeves
never do."* is called "the most relatable line in the set" in
`social-playbook.md` with **nothing behind it** — no customer, no research. The
founder, who is in the target market, had never experienced it. An assertion
repeated until it looks like a fact is the same failure as a stale doc. **Check
who said it before building on a line.**

**Hooks**

- *It never fits in two places at once.* (The chest. The waist. The length.)
- *We do not make a medium. We make the shape of you.*
- *A size chart is an average of thousands of women. It is nobody.*
- *You already pay a tailor. You just pay them second.*
- *Every shape of you.*
- *Your size is never sold out.*

**On "every shape of you":** it is an ordinary English phrase and the founder
means it as one. A previous draft of this file attached a music-licensing
warning to it. There was no song in the brief, nobody proposed using one, and
the caveat read as obstruction. Use the line.

⚠️ **Meta rejects second-person body references in paid.** "You have never been
a medium" is organic only. The ad-safe version is third person: "Nobody is a
medium." This is already documented in `social-playbook.md` and it keeps being
rediscovered.

## 3. Noor, who wants to choose her own sleeve

**Pillar: clothes fitting, the coverage half of it.**

She wants an elbow sleeve, or a full one, or a longer hem, and she is tired of
two bad options: a rail where everything is sleeveless and cropped, or a
"modest" line styled as religious wear when she was shopping for a shirt.

**The customizer already solves this exactly, and we have never once said so.**
She moves the sleeve slider and the photograph changes to the garment she will
receive. That is the single most under-used asset in this brand.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fears** | Being sorted into a category she did not pick |
| **Believes** | Coverage means shopping in a different shop |
| **We move her to** | Coverage is a slider on a normal shirt |
| **Proof we own** | The photo changes. Not a swatch, the actual garment. |

**Hooks**

- *Choose the sleeve. The photo changes to the shirt you are getting.*
- *Same shirt. Four ways to cut it. All the same price.*
- *Not a category. A setting.*

⚠️ **Never use the words "modest wear" or any religious framing.** The offer is
choice. The moment it is named as a category we lose her and we take on a
positioning nobody asked for. **Founder's call to confirm**, because this is
the one persona that is a decision rather than an observation.

⚠️ And no catalogue photograph shows a sleeve worn **down**. Every long-sleeve
shot is rolled. Do not caption one "long sleeve".

## 4. Maryam, who wants to know who made it

**Pillar: made in the UAE.**

She has quit Shein and felt good about it. She follows UAE brands. She wants to
know a name. She is also completely immune to the word "sustainable", because
every brand that ever lied to her used it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fears** | Being sold a story. Greenwashing she cannot check. |
| **Believes** | "Sustainable" is marketing |
| **We move her to** | Do not tell her it is sustainable. Tell her what happens, and let her draw it. |
| **Proof we own** | Nothing is cut before it is bought. No stock, no markdowns, no unsold rail. Sewn here by one tailor we can name. |

**Hooks**

- *Nothing here exists yet. We cut it after you buy it.*
- *No warehouse. No sale rail. Nothing to burn at the end of the season.*
- *Cut and sewn in the UAE, by a tailor with a name.*

⚠️ **Do not volunteer where the cloth is milled.** It was drafted as a line here
and the founder cut it on sight, 2026-09-12: *"don't say this, it's marketing."*
Confessing a weakness in order to look honest is still a technique, and she can
smell it. If a customer asks, answer plainly. Do not put it in creative.

⚠️ **Waste is a structural claim, not an environmental one.** Made to order
means no deadstock. It does not make linen carbon neutral and we do not imply
it. No certification we do not hold. No number we cannot source.

# Where they overlap, and the one line that gets all four

The four personas are not four campaigns. Reem and Maryam are often the same
woman. So is Aisha and Noor.

**The bridge:** *she is paying attention to what goes on her body, and clothing
is the last place she has not looked.*

One line that carries all three pillars without a price, a fibre lie, or an
overreach:

> **Linen, cut to your shape, sewn in the UAE after you order it.**

Everything in that sentence is true of the shipped product. Use it as the
closing frame of any video and as the bio line.

# The claim rules, in one place

Applies to every asset built from this document.

| Never | Instead |
|---|---|
| 100% plant based | 100% linen (there are buttons and a zip fly) |
| 100% made in the UAE | Cut and sewn in the UAE |
| ~~100% tailored~~ **struck 2026-09-13** | **Allowed. Say it.** Nothing is cut before it is ordered, so a standard size is still cut individually by a tailor for that order. There is no stock and therefore no un-tailored path, and the fitting is free to everyone. This row used to ban the phrase and was wrong about the business for weeks. |
| Long sleeve | Rolled sleeve, or name the setting |
| Hypoallergenic, cures, prevents | Breathable, absorbent, a plant fibre |
| Sustainable, eco, green | Made after you order it. No stock, no markdowns. |
| Modest wear | Choose the sleeve |
| Any price | Nothing. Pricing is live. |

House rules carried from the rest of this folder: no em dashes, no urgency, no
countdowns, no discount codes, no invented proof, and the word "AI" never
appears.
