The four text tiles read as one sentence across the grid:

> **CUT — FOR YOUR BODY — NOT — A SIZE CHART**

**Alternatives:**
- `SHAKLEK / شكلك / MEANS / YOUR SHAPE` — teaches the name; softer positioning
- `MADE / TO YOUR MEASURE / IN COTTON / AND LINEN` — most literal, works hardest on cold traffic

**Text-tile design system** (posts 2, 4, 6, 8): solid flat colour, no imagery,
no texture. Light serif, all caps, generously letterspaced, dead centre, set
small — about one-eighth of the tile height. One 1px gold `#9c8445` hairline
rule ~60px wide, centred a third below the text. Text occupies no more than the
middle 50% of the tile. Nothing else.

Palette: Ivory `#f5f0e8` · Navy `#0a2d4a` · Burgundy `#4a1a2d` · gold `#9c8445`

## ⚠️ Which of these to shoot rather than source

**Safe as stock:** landscapes and abstract draped fabric. Nobody reads a dune
as a factual claim about the business, and the Al Qudra photo is even true —
it is the UAE.

**Borderline:** linen macros and folded stacks. Abstract enough to use, but the
moment a caption says *"this is the linen we cut"* while the photo is someone
else's cloth, that is a claim you cannot back. Use brand-mood captions only
until you shoot your own fabric — a phone macro in window light is a 20-minute
job and makes every future post honest.

**Shoot, don't source — the atelier still life (post 5).** This is the clearest
"our workshop" implication, and for a brand whose entire pitch is the making, a
stock atelier comes close to misrepresenting the product. Your tailor's actual
table, on a phone in afternoon light, will convince more than any stock image
precisely because it is real.

**A note on generated images.** Midjourney can produce a convincing linen
macro. Do not use one for fabric or atelier shots: the brand's whole claim is
that real cloth is cut by a real tailor, and a generated fabric shot is the one
lie that undermines the pitch.

## ⚠️ The linen "frequency" claim — deliberately not used

One competitor post claims linen *"has a frequency of 5000Hz"* and that wearing
it *"elevates your frequency, promoting healing."* **None of that appears above
and it should never be posted.** Fabrics have no healing frequency; the 5000Hz
figure traces to a single non-replicated, methodologically unsound source that
circulates as wellness folklore. Publishing it would be a false health claim to
paying customers — a consumer-protection exposure in the UAE and a direct
breach of the brand's own no-claim-without-evidence rule.

When a competitor uses it, that is a reason to trust them less, not a gap in
our content.

The honest case is strong enough: linen's open weave and moisture-wicking flax
fibre keep it wearable in Gulf heat; it softens with every wash; cotton is soft,
absorbent, easy-care and holds a tailored line; polyester genuinely is a
plastic-derived fibre that holds warmth and odour against the skin. All
physically defensible comfort claims — no biology, no healing, no frequencies.
