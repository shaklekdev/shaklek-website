# Waitlist campaign 3–10 Nov 2026: creatives built backward from the customer

Founder, 2026-11-05: "think about our value to the market, the people we're
targeting, why we target them and why they would click. For each creative set
the persona and the why, and work backward from our customer, not from what we
have." The first proposal recycled organic posts; this replaces it.

## Who pays AED 449–949 for a linen shirt, and why us

Affluent women in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, 25–55, who already pay a premium for
what is natural and well made (clean beauty, organic food, wellness), and who
fight fit or coverage in ordinary shops. Personas: `personas.md`.

What we offer that the market does not, together: 100% linen (a plant, not a
blend), cut after the order to a size or her measurements at the same price,
the sleeve/length/colour chosen on a photo that changes, made in the UAE.

Why anyone clicks a cold ad: she recognises herself in the first second, sees
the product, and gets a reason to act now. Ours: opening 11·11, and the first
50 orders get their initials embroidered on the Shaklek bag.

## One creative per persona, in one ad set

Meta shows each creative to whoever responds. After 72 hours the per-ad cost per
lead tells us which persona is our real market. That is worth as much as the
leads.

The offer closes every creative: "First 50 orders: your initials on the
Shaklek bag. Opening 11·11." CTA: Sign up.

Meta rules: no second-person body or health claims ("your skin", "your
body", "non-toxic"), so these hooks speak in the third person or about the
product.
