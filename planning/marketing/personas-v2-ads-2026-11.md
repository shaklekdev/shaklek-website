## What the research found, and did not

- **Reddit was not reachable** (r/dubai, r/UAE block our crawler). Beyond the founder's feedback, no first-person UAE customer quotes were found: every scene below is inferred from sourced facts. Check them with her before filming.
- **Tailors [S]:** The National: you "may need to make multiple trips due to alterations needed or unforeseen delays"; "you could show up... only to learn that the fabric is still sitting, uncut, in the bag"; a Bur Dubai tailoring manager: "A tailor's interpretation of a design from a picture might not always meet a client's expectations." (1)
- **Copies [S]:** Dubai residents ask Satwa/Karama/Bur Dubai tailors to recreate designer pieces; result "will look similar from a distance"; rush jobs 20–50% extra (2).
- **"Linen" on the high street [S]:** H&M "linen-blend" shirts list 55% linen / 45% viscose, some 30% linen / 70% viscose (3). A Zara viscose-linen suit lists 22% linen (4, search snippet).
- **Real 100% linen is already sold here [S]:** Massimo Dutti UAE 100% linen shirts AED 380–500, sale items down to 150 (5, search snippet; page blocked us). COS cotton-linen overshirt AED 545 at Ounass (6). **So 449 is in range; our edge is not the fibre alone.**
- **Linen objection [S]:** a woman advising on UAE clothes: linen trousers, "as they crease so badly!" (7).
- **AC [S]:** "indoors it's absolutely FREEZING due to aircon" (7); The National: "at work, the AC is turned up high; outside, it's scorching... keep a scarf handy" (8).
- **Coverage [S]:** Dubai stylists say covered outfits are easy to find at Zara/Massimo Dutti; The Muslim Stylist: "It could just be a few more inches that you cover" (9). **Coverage is not scarce; the exact inches are.** UAE government advises modest dress in malls (10).
- **Local [S]:** NielsenIQ 2022: 66% of UAE consumers prefer locally made products from small businesses, 54% would pay a little more (11). No fashion-specific data found.

## The four, sharpened

### 1. Aisha, who already has a tailor. Pillar: fit.
- **Who [I]:** 30–45, Dubai, works, has a Karama or Satwa tailor on WhatsApp. Buys at Massimo Dutti, Zara, Ounass, then pays for alterations on top.
- **Scene [I on S]:** Saturday, second trip to the tailor for the same trousers. The waist is still loose because the hips fit [F]; or the cloth is "still sitting, uncut, in the bag" (1).
- **Tried:** sizing up then altering (pays twice); having a tailor copy a favourite ("similar from a distance") (2).
- **Job:** a piece that fits in two places at once, without the runaround.
- **Objection:** "If I send my measurements and it comes out wrong, I paid 519 for nothing."
- **Shaklek:** cut after the order to a standard size or her measurements, **same price**; size guide on site explains how to measure. ⚠️ See Founder decisions on remakes.

### 2. Reem, who already buys "linen". Pillar: fabric.
- **Who [I]:** 28–45, label reader (skincare, food). Owns several "linen" shirts from the high street.
- **Scene [I on S]:** July, 4pm, in a car park after a mall. The "linen" shirt is clinging. That evening she reads the label: 55% linen, 45% viscose (3).
- **Tried:** high-street "linen-blend" (3); Massimo Dutti 100% linen, in their sizes and sleeves (5).
- **Job:** real linen she does not have to check, in her cut.
- **Objection:** "Massimo sells 100% linen for 380. And linen creases." (5, 7)
- **Shaklek:** 100% linen, not a blend; and unlike the rail, cut after the order to her size or measurements, sleeve and length chosen. Creases: own it, do not deny it.

### 3. Noor, who wants the exact inches. Pillar: choice.
- **Refined [S]:** covered clothes are easy to find (9). What she cannot find is *this* sleeve on *this* shirt in *this* fibre; today she solves it by layering a cardigan or scarf over the wrong piece, partly for AC (7, 8, 9).
- **Who [I]:** 25–50, Emirati, Arab or expat, wears sleeves for coverage, for the office AC, or both. Shops Massimo Dutti, Ounass, abaya boutiques.
- **Scene [I on S]:** in a changing room, the shirt is right except the sleeve. She buys it anyway and adds it to the list of things worn with a cardigan (9).
- **Job:** choose the sleeve and length on a normal shirt, trousers, dress or abaya, and know what arrives.
- **Objection:** "The photo online is never what arrives."
- **Shaklek:** the photo changes to the exact garment for the chosen sleeve, length, leg width and colour. ⚠️ No catalogue shot shows a sleeve worn down; never caption "long sleeve".

### 4. Maryam, who wants it made here. Pillar: made in the UAE.
- **Who [I]:** 30–55, buys from UAE brands, prefers local and pays a little more for it (11).
- **Scene [I]:** a fast-fashion parcel that did not fit, unreturned, in a drawer. Inferred only; no source.
- **Job:** a piece with a maker she could name.
- **Objection:** "Made in UAE how? The fabric is from somewhere."
- **Shaklek:** cut and sewn in the UAE after the order; nothing made before it is bought. Never "100% made in UAE"; never volunteer the mill.
- **Thinnest evidence of the four**: better as a line inside other ads than as a cold hook.
