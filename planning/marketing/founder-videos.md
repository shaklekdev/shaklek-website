# Founder videos: one post in three (2026-11-12)

Founder decision, 2026-11-12: **one post in every three is a video of Nada
talking to camera.** Personal branding: the why, the how, the story behind
Shaklek. This file is everything she needs to film them.

Written by shaklek-marketing. Every fact below was checked against the site or
the session log on 2026-11-12 and the source is named. **Prices and the number
of pieces are not stored here**: read `website/src/data/catalog.ts` on the day
of filming if an episode mentions one. Anything only she knows is marked
`[À REMPLIR PAR TOI: ...]`. Nothing personal has been invented.

Rules that apply to every episode: no em dashes in captions or on-screen text,
never the word "AI", no customer numbers, no reviews, no discount, no medical
detail (see section 2), no "the first" (that claim is site only,
`website/src/data/homeContent.ts:41-46`).

## 1. The rhythm

Count posts, not days. **Posts 3, 6, 9, 12 and so on are founder videos.** The
two posts between them are the brand's usual posts (reel, carousel, tile).
Same file to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, as always. These are real
footage, so no AI-generated label.

The pillar rule from `narrative-pillars.md` still holds: the founder video
never repeats the subject of the post before or after it. The order of
episodes below was chosen for that (for example "Why linen" does not sit next
to the linen carousel, and "How an order works" does not sit next to the
All included tile).

## 3. The bank: ten episodes

30 to 60 seconds each. **Talking points, not a script.** Read them once, put
the phone down, say it your way. The hook is the only line worth learning by
heart. Language: `[À REMPLIR PAR TOI: English, Arabic, French, or a mix? The
hooks are written in English; on-screen text follows whatever you speak.]`

Hooks open on a memory or a plain fact, never on what is wrong with the
viewer (her rule).

### Ep 1. Why I started

- **Hook, spoken:** "I work in tech. And I opened a clothing label. Here is why."
- **On screen:** `WHY I STARTED SHAKLEK`
- **Talking points:**
  - Years of caring about everything that touched my skin, and clothes were
    the one thing worn all day (beat 1). No medical detail.
  - Whenever I found the piece, it was synthetic, not my taste, or not my fit
    (beat 2). `[À REMPLIR: the piece that almost worked]`
  - The question I kept asking: show me every version before it exists, then
    make only the one I chose (beat 4).
  - So that is what we built: you change the piece on screen, and we make
    that one, in linen.
- **Ending:** "We opened on the eleventh of November. Come and see. The link
  is in the bio."
- **Persona:** Reem first, all four. **Pillar:** 9 Founder speaks (story of 7).

### Ep 2. What Shaklek means

- **Hook, spoken:** "Shaklek is an Arabic word." (say it: شكلك) "It means
  your way."
- **On screen:** `شكلك` then `SHAKLEK = YOUR WAY`
- **Talking points:**
  - The word also carries shape and look: your look, your way.
  - Why that name: the piece is decided by the person wearing it, not by us.
    The sleeve, the length, the colour.
  - `[À REMPLIR: who found the name, and the other names you rejected, if you
    want to tell it]`
  - The line from the site: "Your look, your vision, simply you."
- **Ending:** "That is the whole idea. Shaklek, your way."
- **Persona:** Noor, Maryam. **Pillar:** 9 (story of 7).
- **Note:** the brand name on screen is the Arabic word and the master logo,
  never the name typed in another font.

### Ep 3. Nobody is a size

- **Hook, spoken:** "Think of the one piece in your wardrobe that fits you
  perfectly. I bet you can count them on one hand."
- **On screen:** `NOBODY'S SIZE IS M`
- **Talking points:**
  - A size chart is an average of everybody (the approved line from the
    "Nobody's size is" reel).
  - `[À REMPLIR: your own fit story. What never fits you? Waist, sleeves,
    length?]`
  - What we do instead: we take your measurements and the piece is made to
    them. Or pick a standard size, XS to XXL. The price is the same either
    way (`faq/page.tsx:66-85`).
  - And if it still needs a touch, one alteration is included within 10 days
    of delivery (`faq/page.tsx:91-105`). Say "alteration", never "remake" or
    "refund".
- **Ending:** "Made to your measurements. Link in the bio."
- **Persona:** Aisha. **Pillar:** 9 (subject of 1).
- **Organic only.** Do not boost: Meta rejects creative that speaks about the
  viewer's body.

### Ep 4. Why only linen

- **Hook, spoken:** "We work with one fabric. Only one. People told me that
  was a mistake."
  `[À REMPLIR: did someone really tell you that? If not, use: "We work with
  one fabric. Only one. Here is why."]`
- **On screen:** `ONE FABRIC. 100% LINEN.`
- **Talking points:**
  - Linen is flax, a plant. It absorbs moisture, gives it up again, and lets
    air through (`our-story/page.tsx:126`).
  - In a country with months above forty degrees, you feel that by the
    afternoon (same line).
  - It creases. That is what linen does, and we do not hide it. It also gets
    softer every time it is washed (`faq/page.tsx:130-134`).
  - It is what I wanted against my own skin. Hold the cloth up to the camera.
- **Ending:** "One fabric, done properly. That is our choice."
- **Persona:** Reem. **Pillar:** 9 (subject of 3).
- **Do not say:** any health or skin-condition claim, "hypoallergenic",
  "16 hours a day" (no source), or "100% natural" about the whole garment.
  The claim is about the fabric: "100% linen".

### Ep 5. How an order works

- **Hook, spoken:** "You order online. And then we come to your home."
- **On screen:** `YOU ORDER. WE COME TO YOU.` small: `IN DUBAI`
- **Talking points:**
  - You choose the piece on the site and customise it. The photo changes as
    you choose.
  - After you order, we message you on WhatsApp and arrange a private home
    fitting in Dubai. It is included in the price. Outside Dubai we guide you
    to take the measurements, or you pick a standard size.
  - Our tailor makes your piece. Allow up to three weeks: it does not exist
    before you order it.
  - We deliver across the UAE, included. One alteration is included too.
- **Ending:** "All included in the price. Link in the bio."
- **Persona:** Aisha (her "what if it's wrong"). **Pillar:** 9 (subject of 8).
- **Voice:** this whole episode is "we", even if she does a fitting herself.
  Never "book a fitting": she orders first, then we contact her; there is no
  booking system. Sources: `homeContent.ts:148`, `faq/page.tsx:31-85`,
  `included.ts:19-25`.

### Ep 6. With our tailor

- **Hook, spoken:** (in the atelier, a piece on the table) "This did not
  exist last week. Somebody ordered it, so we are making it."
- **On screen:** `MADE AFTER YOU ORDER`
- **Talking points:**
  - Nothing is made before it is ordered. No stock, nothing left over.
  - Show one real step: the chalk, the shears, the machine. Let the sound run
    for two seconds without talking.
  - `[À REMPLIR: how you found the tailor, years of experience, the thing
    they are fussy about. First name only with their yes.]`
  - Each piece is cut and sewn for one person, one at a time.
- **Ending:** "Handmade by our team, in the UAE."
- **Persona:** Maryam. **Pillar:** 9 (subject of 6).
- **Before filming:** ask the tailor's permission, on camera or hands only.
  Do not show another customer's name, measurements or order sheet. **Never
  say how many tailors there are** (standing rule, `homeContent.ts:353-370`):
  "our tailor" or "our team", no number.

### Ep 7. Three fabric myths

- **Hook, spoken:** "Three things I believed about fabric before I started
  this. All three were wrong."
  `[À REMPLIR: only if true of you. Otherwise: "Three things about fabric
  nobody tells you in the shop."]`
- **On screen:** `MYTH / FACT`
- **Talking points:**
  - Myth: "The label says linen, so it is linen." Fact: read the composition.
    High-street "linen blend" can be around half linen, the rest viscose
    (`personas-v2-ads-2026-11.md`, source 3). **Name no brand on camera.**
  - Myth: "Polyester is just another fabric." Fact: it is made from
    petroleum. It is plastic. It holds heat in and holds on to smell
    (`our-story/page.tsx:126`).
  - Myth: "If it creases, it is poor quality." Fact: creasing is what real
    linen does.
- **Ending:** "Tonight, read one label in your wardrobe. Tell me what it
  says." (a comment prompt, which is the point)
- **Persona:** Reem. **Pillar:** 9 (subject of 3).
- **Do not say:** anything about illness, rashes, bacteria or toxins. Comfort
  and fibre facts only. **Do not claim there is nothing synthetic in a Shaklek
  piece**: the sewing thread is polyester (session log, 2026-11-07, "One
  fabric, not one ingredient"). Organic only, do not boost.

### Ep 8. What "made in the UAE" means for us

- **Hook, spoken:** "Made in the UAE. Let me tell you exactly what we mean by

- **On screen:** `MADE IN THE UAE` then `CUT HERE. SEWN HERE.`

- **Ending:** "Made here, worn here."
- **Persona:** Maryam. **Pillar:** 9 (subject of 4).
- **Never:** "100% made in the UAE" (the cloth is woven abroad), "the first",
  or "local fabric". **Her call before filming:** whether to say out loud

  The standing rule is never to name the mill or its country unprompted

### Ep 9. What we got wrong

- **Hook, spoken:** "We moved our opening date. Twice."
- **On screen:** `WHAT WE GOT WRONG`
- **Talking points (pick ONE story, all four are true and on record):**

## 4. Filming guide

**The idea: a person talking, not an advert.** Restraint is the brand. The
reference brands spend money on the product photographs and nothing on the
founder's phone video, and that contrast is why it is believed.

**Camera**
- Your phone, back camera if someone holds it or it sits on a stand, front
  camera if you are alone. Vertical 9:16. Clean the lens.
- Phone at eye level, an arm's length away. Eyes in the upper third of the
  frame, head and shoulders, a little space above your head. Look at the
  lens, not at yourself on the screen.
- Keep the bottom quarter and the right edge of the frame empty of anything
  important: the apps cover them with their buttons and caption.

**Light and sound**
- Natural light only. Face a window, or stand in open shade outdoors. Never
  with the window behind you, never under a ceiling spotlight, never in
  midday sun (the note from the 8 Nov shoot: next time, open shade).
- Sound matters more than picture. A quiet room, AC off for the take, phone
  close. In the atelier, stop the machines while you speak, then let them run.
- No trending song. Original sound only (business account rule,
  `tiktok-launch.md`).

**Takes**
- One take. Three at the very most. If the third is not right, change what
  you are saying, not how you say it. The second take is usually the one.
- Do not read. Glance at the talking points, put them away, talk to one
  friend.
- A small stumble stays in. A restart mid-sentence stays in.

**Length and edit**
- 30 to 60 seconds. If it runs past 60, it is two episodes.
- The hook is spoken in the first 2 seconds, with nothing before it. No
  "hi guys", no intro, no logo first.
- Hook also on screen for the first 3 seconds, upper third, house text style
  (`text-on-image-style.md`).
- Subtitles burned into the video, the whole way through, large and plain.
  Most people watch without sound. Check the subtitles for errors, brand name
  first.
- No filter, no beauty mode, no transitions, no stickers. A straight cut
  between sentences is enough. Master logo on the last second only.
- Send the file and we add hook text, subtitles and the end card, then check
  the exported file before it goes up.

**Where**
- **Al Fahidi:** early morning or the last hour before sunset, a quiet lane,
  the sand-coloured wall behind you. Good for episodes 2, 8.
- **The atelier:** episode 6, and a strong setting for 3 and 4. Ask first.
  Nothing with another customer's details in frame.
- **Home:** by a window, plain wall or the linen itself behind you. Good for
  episodes 1, 5, 7, 9, 10. Tidy only what is in the frame.

**What you wear**
- A Shaklek piece, every time. A different piece or colour each episode so
  the feed does not repeat. Linen that has been worn a little: a soft crease
  is the fabric being honest.
- Say nothing about it. The caption can name the piece.

**What not to do**
- No script read aloud, no teleprompter.
- No "I" for what the company does (section 5).
- No medical detail, no diagnosis, no health claim for linen.
- No customer numbers, no "so many of you", no reviews we do not have.
- No discount, no code, no "last chance". The launch gift may be mentioned
  as a gift (first 50 orders), never as a deal.
- No "AI", no "the first", no "100% made in the UAE", no number of tailors,
  no faster delivery than the site says.
- No competitor or other brand by name.
- No filming in the car, no walking selfie, no ring light, no props.
- Do not redo it five times. Unpolished and true beats smooth.

## 5. Voice: "I" for her story, "we" for the brand

**There is a conflict, and it is direct.** `.claude/agents/shaklek-marketing.md`
(founder rule, 2026-11-12) says every customer-facing communication speaks as
"we", "never a solo-founder voice, even in a warm or personal email". A founder
talking to camera about why she started cannot be told in "we" without
sounding false. `narrative-pillars.md` had already flagged the same thing for
pillar 7.

The two rules were made the same day for two different jobs, and they can
both hold:

| She says "I" for | She says "we" for |
|---|---|
| Her own past: "I spent years...", "I kept asking..." | Everything the company does today |
| Her own taste and reasons: "I wanted...", "I chose linen because..." | Who comes to the fitting: "we come to you" |
| Who she is: "I work in tech" | Who makes it: "our tailor", "our team" |
| Her own mistakes and lessons | Who answers, delivers, alters: "we" |

**The test:** if the sentence is about her life or her reasons, "I". If it is
about what a customer will receive, "we". The reason for the "we" rule was
that a customer must never think one woman alone will turn up, sew and
deliver. That reason is untouched: on camera she is the founder of a team,
not the whole company.

Never, even on camera: "I'll come to you", "I make each piece", "I answer
every message", "my little brand", "it's just me".

This is not new. Her published note on `/our-story` already works exactly
this way: "I'm a tech girl..." and "That's what I built", next to "before we
even take your measurements".

**Proposed change to the agent rule, for her yes (not made, the agent file is
hers to change):**

> One exception: when the founder speaks in her own person (a video to
> camera, her signed note on /our-story), she says "I" for her own story and
> reasons, and "we" for everything the brand does, makes or promises.
> Captions under those videos stay "we", or quote her.

Site copy, emails, ads, WhatsApp templates and bios stay "we" with no
exception.
