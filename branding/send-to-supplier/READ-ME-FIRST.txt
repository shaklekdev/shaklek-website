FOUR NOTES ON THOSE FILES

  The hang tag is DOUBLE SIDED. 03 is the front, 03b is the back. Same card,
  same size, printed both sides.

  The business card is DOUBLE SIDED and is the only item here supplied WITH
  BLEED. The two PDFs measure 96 x 56 mm because 3 mm of bleed is included on
  every edge. Trim to 90 x 50 mm. The front prints edge to edge in near-black,
  and without that bleed a small drift on the guillotine leaves a white line
  down one side.

  TWO ITEMS CARRY A QR CODE: 05-thank-you-card-back and 10b-business-card-back.
  Please print them exactly as supplied. Do not redraw, re-generate, re-colour,
  scale one axis, or place anything over them, and do not reduce them below the
  size in the file. The blank margin around each code is part of the code, not
  a margin we left for tidiness -- crop into it and phones stop reading it. If
  a code has to move or change size, please ask us for a new file.

  07-tissue-wrap: this is one tile. Step and repeat it across the 50 x 70 cm
  sheet. Keep it faint: it should read as texture, not as print.

THREE THINGS

  1. Please do not retype the name in a font. These files are the artwork.
  2. The logo CANNOT be embroidered. Its thin strokes are far below the
     minimum for satin stitch. Use shaklek-monogram.pdf for anything stitched.
     The PDF explains why.
  3. Please send a physical proof on the real material before any production
     run, especially for the gold.
