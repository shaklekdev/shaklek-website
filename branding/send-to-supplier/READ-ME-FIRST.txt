TWO NOTES ON THOSE FILES

  03-hang-tag: "A7F3C210" is a SPECIMEN, not a fixed value. The order number
  is different on every garment. It shows you where the field sits and how big
  it is. Please quote for variable data on this item.

  07-tissue-wrap: this is one tile. Step and repeat it across the 50 x 70 cm
  sheet. Keep it faint: it should read as texture, not as print.

THREE THINGS

  1. Please do not retype the name in a font. These files are the artwork.
  2. The logo CANNOT be embroidered. Its thin strokes are far below the
     minimum for satin stitch. Use shaklek-monogram.pdf for anything stitched.
     The PDF explains why.
  3. Please send a physical proof on the real material before any production
     run, especially for the gold.

Other formats (JPG, the name on its own, the Arabic on its own) are in
