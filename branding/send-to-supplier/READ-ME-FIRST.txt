NOTES ON THOSE FILES

  1. THE HANG TAG IS DOUBLE SIDED. 03 is the front, 03b is the back. Same card,
     same size, printed both sides.

  2. THE THANK-YOU CARD IS LANDSCAPE: 148 mm WIDE x 105 mm TALL. It is the same
     A6 sheet as a portrait card, turned on its side, so the paper and the price
     do not move. It is landscape to match the envelope: the card slides
     straight in and comes straight out the right way up, instead of having to
     be turned ninety degrees each way.

  3. THE ENVELOPE IS C6 LANDSCAPE, 162 mm WIDE x 114 mm TALL, and it must be a
     WALLET envelope — the flap hinged on the LONG edge, pointed, opening
     across the top. That is the card and invitation format. A POCKET envelope
     is the same C6 sheet with the flap on the short edge, used portrait, and
     it is what invoices and statements come in: same paper, same price, wrong
     object.

     ONLY THE BACK PRINTS. The front carries nothing at all — this envelope
     never travels on its own, it sits inside the linen bag inside the mailer,
     so it needs no address panel and no stamp box. The monogram sits on the
     back, 9 mm, below the point of the flap and NOT on the flap itself: on a
     gummed flap the ink meets the adhesive, and under a self-seal strip it is
     covered. IN THE ARTWORK FILE THE TINTED TRIANGLE IS THE FLAP AND DOES NOT
     PRINT.

     C6 is a shelf size everywhere, so there is no die to pay for. The card at
     148 x 105 goes in with about 7 mm of clearance along the long edge and
     4.5 mm along the short one.

  4. THE BUSINESS CARD IS DOUBLE SIDED and is the only item here supplied WITH
     BLEED. Both PDFs measure 96 x 56 mm because 3 mm of bleed is included on
     every edge. TRIM TO 90 x 50 mm. The front prints edge to edge in
     near-black, and without that bleed a small drift on the guillotine leaves
     a white line down one side. No crop marks needed.

  5. THE COTTON BAG IS LANDSCAPE: 500 mm WIDE x 400 mm TALL, wider than it is
     tall. 08-linen-bag-print.pdf is the print area at that size. The wordmark
     is 110 mm WIDE, one colour, centred, at about a third of the bag height.

  6. THE MAILER IS A MINIMUM, NOT A SPECIFICATION: 320 x 400 mm internal, kraft
     paper at 120 gsm or heavier, with a self-seal strip. Your nearest stock
     size at or above that is the right answer and the cheapest one — please do
     not cut a custom size for this. The monogram is fixed in millimetres from
     the lower-right corner, so the placement stays correct at any bag size.

  7. TWO ITEMS CARRY A QR CODE: 05-thank-you-card-back and
     10b-business-card-back. Please print them exactly as supplied. Do not
     redraw, re-generate, re-colour, scale one axis, or place anything over
     them, and do not reduce them below the size in the file. The blank margin
     around each code is part of the code, not a margin we left for tidiness —
     crop into it and phones stop reading it. If a code has to move or change
     size, please ask us for a new file.

  8. THE TISSUE WRAP FILE IS ONE TILE. Step and repeat it across the
     50 x 70 cm sheet. Keep it faint: it should read as texture, not as print.

HOW THE PARCEL GOES TOGETHER

  Asked and answered, because a list of parts does not say how the parts nest.
  Five layers, outside in:

    1. kraft mailer          320 x 400 mm min, 120 gsm+, self-seal
    2. linen drawstring bag  50 x 40 cm LANDSCAPE <- the box, and she keeps it
    3. tissue wrap           two sheets, 50 x 70 cm, closed with the gold seal
    4. the garment           folded, with its labels and hang tag
    5. thank-you card in its C6 landscape envelope, plus a business card

  THE KRAFT IS THE OUTER MAILER, NOT A WRAPPING PAPER. Nothing is wrapped in
  kraft. The paper that touches the cloth is unbleached tissue.

  Is kraft sturdy enough? Yes. A folded garment is soft goods — no corners,
  nothing rigid, nothing to crush — and kraft paper mailers are the normal
  choice for clothing. The failure is never the panel, it is the seal opening
  or an edge tearing, so the specification is the weight and the closure:
  120 gsm or heavier, full-width self-seal strip, no padded lining. Recycled
  poly is tougher and cheaper and we do not want it; plastic contradicts the
  whole brand. Please send a sample of your 120 gsm kraft and we will confirm
  on the real thing.

THREE THINGS

  1. Please do not retype the name in a font. These files are the artwork.
  2. The logo CANNOT be embroidered. Its thin strokes are far below the
     minimum for satin stitch. Use shaklek-monogram.pdf for anything stitched.
     The PDF explains why.
  3. Please send a physical proof on the real material before any production
     run, especially for the gold.
