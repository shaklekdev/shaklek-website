SHAKLEK — ARTWORK
=================

Everything you need is in this folder. Nothing here needs a font installed.

START WITH:  READ-ME-FIRST-Shaklek-artwork.pdf
             Two pages. It says which mark goes on which item, the colours,
             the minimum sizes, and which process will not work.

THE FILES

  shaklek-logo                    The logo. Use this one unless told otherwise.
  shaklek-logo-one-colour         Same, for single-colour printing.
  shaklek-logo-white-on-dark      For printing onto anything dark.
  shaklek-monogram                The short mark. Small items and embroidery.
  shaklek-monogram-white-on-dark  The same, for dark surfaces.
  shaklek-gold-seal               The round sticker.

  .pdf  Vector. Please use this.
  .png  Transparent, high resolution, if you cannot take vector.

TWO THINGS

  1. Please do not retype the name in a font. These files are the artwork.
  2. The logo cannot be embroidered. Its thin strokes are far below the
     minimum for satin stitch. Use shaklek-monogram.pdf for anything
     stitched. The PDF explains why.

Other formats (JPG, the name on its own, the Arabic on its own) are in
../other-formats/. Ask if you need one.
