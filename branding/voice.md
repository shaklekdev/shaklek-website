# Voice — and the "shhhhh" idea

## The idea

Founder, 2026-08-26. A campaign built on **"shhhhh"** — someone hushing the
camera. Approved as a direction; **not built yet**, deliberately.

## Why it fits Shaklek and not just any brand

**The Arabic letter ش is the "sh" sound.** It is the first letter of شكلك and it
is now the brand's monogram (`logo/shaklek-monogram-black.svg`). So the mark on
the garment and the noise in the video are the same thing. Most brands have to
invent that link; this one already had it sitting in its own name.

It also matches the tone the site already uses: quiet, unhurried, the opposite
of a sale.

## What it actually means

It works because it is a **secret**, not a joke. The piece nobody else has, the
one cut for you. That is true of what Shaklek sells, which is what stops it
being a gag that wears out in a month.

- **Lines it can carry:** "Shhh. It's made for you." · "Nobody else has this
  one." · A finger to the lips, and the ش appearing.
- **Where it belongs:** Reels and Stories. It is a *spoken* idea — it does
  almost nothing on a still image or a static ad.

## The one rule

⚠️ **Never use it for a discount.** "Shhh, secret sale" spends the idea on the
cheapest possible use and cannot be taken back. If it is ever going to mean
"this was made for you", it must never have meant "20% off".

## Why it is not built yet

It needs a real reel, a real voice and a real garment reveal, and it is worth
far more once there is a customer to film it for. Take the mark now; come back
to the campaign when there is something to be quiet about.

## Two standing corrections, from earlier sessions

Both are founder corrections, already enforced in `scripts/social/kinda-chic.mjs`,
which refuses to render copy that breaks them:

- **No em dashes** in customer-facing copy.
- **Never say "AI"**, and never say "photograph" of a generated image.

## The wording that is not a copy target

The founder's own definition of the brand, kept verbatim because it has been
"improved" back into something worse twice:

> *"timeless fashion essentials. This is the brand, this is our niche. People
> need to understand that these are fashion essentials: timeless, elegant,
> customizable, very skin-friendly, organic cotton or linen."*

The hero line is hers too: **"Customisable pieces for you, by you, always at the
same price."** She wrote the "always"; it replaced "at the same price either
way", where "either way" was doing the work of a footnote.
