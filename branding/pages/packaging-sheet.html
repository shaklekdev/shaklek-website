Shaklek · packaging

What arrives at her door

Nine elements, drawn at their real proportions and in their real colours. Every piece a customer keeps carries the full wordmark — Latin, gold rule, Arabic — the same lockup as the site header.

One decision sets the rest, so it is first.

The bag, not a box

A rigid box is what a shop uses to make a stocked item feel special. Shaklek is one tailor making one piece for one person, and a reusable linen bag says that — then keeps saying it every time she uses it for something else. It is also roughly half the cost and survives a courier better.

Because there is no box, the tissue wrap stops being optional. It is the only thing separating a finished garment from a shipping mailer.

The nine elements

Prices are UAE ranges for a first run and need real quotes. The two worth negotiating hardest are the linen bag and the woven label — together they are two thirds of the cost, and both fall sharply with quantity.

On a 389 AED shirt at in-store linen, packaging at 16.50 moves the margin from 58% to about 54%. A rigid box instead of the linen bag would take it to roughly 51%.

That is not an argument against doing it. Before she wears the piece, the packaging is most of what she can actually judge — and this brand has no reviews and no press to do that job instead. It is an argument for pricing it honestly, once.

Before ordering anything

  1. Confirm the required care wording for the UAE. Fibre content, care and country of origin are very likely a legal disclosure, not a courtesy — and linen shrinks, so a missing instruction turns the first wash into a remake you pay for under a returns policy that already promises one.
  2. Three quotes on the linen bag, at 100 and at 200. It is half the cost of the parcel and the price per unit moves the most with quantity.
  3. Ask the woven-label supplier for their true minimum. 500 is the usual floor; some will do 300.
  4. Put the real per-order figure in the pricing file. Friday, with the fabric numbers.
  5. Order one sample of everything before any bulk run. Nobody has held any of this. A wordmark that reads beautifully on a screen can vanish into kraft.
