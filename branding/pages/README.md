# The three visual sheets

Published as private Artifacts on the founder's Claude account, and kept here as
plain HTML so they survive independently of it. Open any of them in a browser.

| File | Live link | What it settled |
|---|---|---|
| `wordmark-trials.html` | https://claude.ai/code/artifact/6d13d123-e8e8-46da-a142-6e6b48557414 | Four typefaces for the wordmark, large and in the real header. **Chose Italiana.** |
| `packaging-sheet.html` | https://claude.ai/code/artifact/d3ec443a-a653-4322-8ab5-759d3049d75d | Nine packaging elements drawn in colour, materials, cost per order. **Chose the linen bag over a box.** |
| `monogram-study.html` | https://claude.ai/code/artifact/175448f7-ccb3-4778-a83b-f0177cc403ef | Five short marks and the "shhhhh" idea. **Chose ش.** |

They pull Italiana, Cormorant Garamond, Reem Kufi and Jost from Google Fonts, so
they need a connection to look right. The decisions they led to are written down
in `../README.md`, `../packaging.md` and `../voice.md` — read those first; these
are the arguments, not the answers.
