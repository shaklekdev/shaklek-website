# Organic content additions, no advertising permit required (2026-08-30)

New organic pieces built from the 26 principles. These EXTEND
instagram-content-pack.md, social-playbook.md and posting-calendar.md; nothing
here duplicates an asset that already exists. Organic posting needs no DET
advertising permit because nothing here advertises a discount or promotion;
there are none to advertise, and stating a normal price is not an offer.

Rules enforced in every line: no em dashes, no "AI", no "photograph" for
generated imagery, no scarcity or stock language, no testimonials or customer
counts (production has two orders), no discount of any kind, no "shhhhh".
Second-person body lines are marked ORGANIC ONLY per the Meta rules in
social-playbook.md.

---

## Series 1: "What happens after you press pay" (the honest substitute for #10)

A brand with no reviews earns trust by showing the process instead (#10, #23,
#15). Three posts or one three-part video, filmed on a phone, real hands, real
studio. Nothing generated, so it can honestly be called filmed.

**Part 1, the order:**
> You press pay. Then a person reads your order. Not a system, a stylist,
> who checks your measurements and your notes and confirms anything unusual
> with you before a single cut is made.

**Part 2, the cut:**
> One tailor, one piece. Yours is the only one being made, because it did not
> exist until you asked for it.

**Part 3, the arrival:**
> About ten working days after your order is confirmed, it arrives anywhere
> in the UAE. Delivery is included. If the fit is off, the first alteration
> or remake is free within 14 days.

Caption for the set:
> No warehouse. No stock. Just your order, read by a person and cut by a
> tailor. This is what made-to-order actually means.

## Series 2: "We are new" (the trust post only a new brand can make)

The absence of proof, said out loud, converts better than proof faked (#23).

> We are new. You have not heard of us, and we are not going to pretend
> otherwise with borrowed reviews and big numbers.
>
> Here is what we can show you instead. A Dubai trade licence on every
> product page. A person who reads every order before anything is cut. A
> tailor in the UAE who makes one piece at a time. And one promise in
> writing: if the fit is not right, the first alteration or remake is free
> within 14 days.
>
> Ask us anything before you order. A person answers.

## Series 3: the five questions, as a carousel (#22)

One slide per question, answer on the same slide, lifted from the site FAQ so
the two can never disagree. Slide order: Will it fit / How long / Can I
return it / Who are you / Why AED 389. Full copy in objection-handling.md.
Closing slide:
> Anything we missed? The answer is a message away.

## Series 4: "What AED 389 buys" (#3, #14)

Stating a price is not advertising an offer. ORGANIC AND AD-SAFE.

> AED 389 is the whole number. The linen. The cut, to your measurements or
> your size, no surcharge either way. Every option you change on the piece.
> Delivery across the UAE. There is nothing to add at checkout, and no code
> to hunt for, because the price is just the price.

("no code to hunt for" is true today and stays true as long as the
no-promotion decision stands; if a code ever exists, retire this line first.)

## Series 5: linen, said the way the customer feels it (#1, #9)

The site's fabric line is a spec. The organic version is the felt result.
ORGANIC AND AD-SAFE, no body references.

> August in the UAE is the honest test of a fabric. Linen breathes when
> synthetics cling, and it softens with every wear instead of wearing out.
> That is why it is the only fabric we cut. 100% linen, nothing else.

Do NOT say: pre-shrinking, dry-clean explanations, hormone claims, or any
care caveat. Care instructions live on the product page where the law wants
them; a feed post that explains a risk nobody asked about reads as a defect
notice (founder decision, recorded in productDisclosure.ts).

## What is deliberately NOT in this file

- Nothing "kinda chic". The format is body positivity refusing luxury, and a
  product line inside it reads as the thing the trend mocks (kinda-chic.md).
  If the founder wants a kinda chic post, use only the SAFE lines already
  cleared there, and re-check the fabric wording first: two of the cleared
  lines still say "cotton or linen" and cotton is not sellable.
- No urgency, no launch countdowns, no "first 100 customers".
- No discount teases of any kind, and never "shhhhh" for one.
