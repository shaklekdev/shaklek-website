# Slip dress — approved 2026-09-20

The plain sleeveless linen dress from `simple-dress-product`. Founder approved
the master with one word: "amazing master".

`APPROVED/` is the shippable set. Six frames, ivory, 2 necks x 2 lengths:

    1-round-maxi-FRONT      2-V-maxi-FRONT      3-maxi-BACK-shared
    4-round-midi-FRONT      5-V-midi-FRONT      6-midi-BACK-shared

## The customisation model

- **NECK** — round, or V ("decolleté like under the abaya"). Her words.
- **LENGTH** — maxi (hem clears the floor) or midi.
- **ALWAYS SLEEVELESS**, both necks, both lengths. Not an option.

⚠️ **THE BACK IS SHARED ACROSS NECKLINES — her call, and it halves the shoot.**
The back is the same plain high curve whichever neck the front has, so neckline
multiplies FRONTS ONLY and length multiplies both: **2 fronts + 1 back per
length = 6 frames per colourway, not 8.** Across four colourways that is 18
recolours rather than 24. Two V-backs were generated before she said this and
are in `rejected/`, kept rather than deleted.

⚠️ **MIDI MATCHES THE ABAYA'S MIDI, DELIBERATELY.** A shorter hem just below the
knee was proposed and she rejected it: *"the midi should be the same as abaya's
length, we're selling sets and i don't agree on the shorter version."* The midi
cells were generated with the abaya's own midi photograph passed in as a hem
reference, fenced to hem height only, rather than described — so the hems line
up when the dress is worn under the abaya.

## Construction, for the tailor sheet this still needs

- Sleeveless, high wide-set armhole with a WIDE strap across the shoulder. Not
  thin straps, not a halter, not a racerback.
- Fitted through bust and waist, skimming, then widening gently below the hip.
  No waist seam, no gathering, no pleats, no belt.
- One plain vertical seam down the centre front. Hem level all the way round.
- Back is ONE clean panel: no centre back seam, no vent, no slit.
- ⚠️ **ZIP IN THE LEFT SIDE SEAM**, underarm to hip, about 45 cm, concealed.
  Linen does not stretch so a fitted dress needs one. Left because you zip it
  with the right hand while the left holds the fabric taut, and the lap sits
  better on the left; right-side closures read oddly. It is a convention, not a
  rule, but there is no reason to break it. It is invisible in every frame, so
  it has to be SAID on the tailor sheet or it will not be made.
- ⚠️ **NO POCKETS on this dress.** It is fitted straight through the hip and in
  linen a pocket bag there bulges. Pockets belong on the buttoned dress, whose
  skirt flares.
