The second of the two new products. Founder: "nice job claude!"

`APPROVED/` is the shippable set. Six frames, ivory:

    1-round-maxi-FRONT      2-collarV-maxi-FRONT      3-maxi-BACK-shared
    4-round-midi-FRONT      5-collarV-midi-FRONT      6-midi-BACK-shared

## What the garment is

Sleeveless button-front dress. Fitted through the bust and closely through the
waist, with a real WAIST SEAM, and the skirt flaring from it into a wide A-line.
Small SELF-COVERED IVORY LINEN buttons — same colour as the cloth, never white,
shell or wood — in a close even row on a narrow flat placket. Side seam pockets.

- **NECK — she chooses.** ROUND: a high collarless neckline with the buttons
  running **right up to it**, the topmost button immediately below the neckline.
  ⚠️ The first master had a small V split above the first button and she
  rejected it: *"buttons should be up until the neck"*.
  COLLAR-V: a proper pointed SHIRT COLLAR lying flat and open, a soft V below
  it, and the buttons starting at the bottom of the V.
- **LENGTH — she chooses.** Maxi (hem clears the floor) or midi.
- **SLEEVELESS always**, high wide-set armhole with a WIDE strap across the
  shoulder. Not thin straps, not a halter, not a racerback.

⚠️ **BACK SHARED ACROSS NECKLINES**, same rule she set on the slip dress: the
back is one plain curve whichever neck the front has, and carries NO buttons.
Neckline multiplies FRONTS only. **6 frames per colourway, not 8.**

⚠️ **MIDI MATCHES THE ABAYA'S MIDI.** Generated with the abaya's own midi
photograph as a hem reference, fenced to hem height only. The two are sold as
sets and the hems have to agree — see the slip dress README for her ruling.

## Pockets

⚠️ **POCKETS ARE STANDARD HERE, NEVER AN OPTION** — as a slider they would
double the shoot, exactly like closures on the abaya. As a fixed feature they
cost nothing in photography.

⚠️ **AND THE POSE IS HOW THEY EXIST.** Her instruction: *"we should show the
pockets by putting model's hands in them in a stylish way."* Both hands in the
pockets, thumbs outside, in every frame including the backs. A side-seam pocket
is invisible in a straight-on photograph otherwise, and an unseen feature is one
nobody is paying for.

Cost, asked for and answered before committing: **about AED 5.40 of cloth** —
two bags, four pieces roughly 30x17cm, ~0.15m of 138cm cloth at 36.08/m. Under
1% of the likely price, and probably less in practice because an A-line skirt
leaves selvedge offcuts a pocket bag can be cut from. Worth confirming with the
tailor, who lays the marker.

⚠️ **NO POCKETS ON THE SLIP DRESS**, and that is not an oversight: it is fitted
straight through the hip, and in linen a pocket bag there bulges. Pockets work
here only because the skirt flares away from the body.
